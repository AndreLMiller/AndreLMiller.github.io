<h1>Working with AI</h1>
<p>I have never worked with AI in my life until I had taken this class, I looked down on it in the beginning thinking that it was a way of cheating and that I would not learn if I were to use it.  Even though it was okay to use AI in the class I was reluctant at first, but watched as my classmates were able to work with AI since they knew how to use it in the past.  I decided that this is the future and that i should learn how to work with AI and use it as a tool to learn.  I began using chatGPT and was very confused when asking it questions, I had to be more specific when it came to asking it a question because it would just give me the wrong answer of what I wanted.</p>
